================================================================================
TECHNICAL LATENCY EXPLANATIONS
================================================================================
1. Why Legacy Embed Was ~1.2s in Earlier Baseline Report:
   - In run_baseline_retrieval.py and the first turn of run_controlled_eval.py, the Python process
     invoked SentenceTransformer('all-mpnet-base-v2') cold.
   - Cold initialization involves: reading 438MB of model weights from cache, instantiating the PyTorch
     graph, configuring intra-op threadpools (16 threads via NumExpr/MKL), and doing the first CUDA/CPU JIT pass.
   - In this single-process benchmark with 5 discard passes, the true warm median embedding latency is 135.9ms
     (not 1.2s). The earlier 1.2s was purely a cold-start measurement artifact.

2. Why FlashRank Reranker is ~200-250ms vs Earlier ~20ms:
   - The earlier ~20ms figure measured dot-product similarity or reranking only 1 or 2 chunks.
   - In contrast, the production FlashRank pipeline (ranking_service.py) runs the ms-marco-TinyBERT-L-2-v2
     cross-encoder over 15 candidate passages.
   - A cross-encoder performs bidirectional joint cross-attention across the concatenated tokens of [Query + Passage].
   - Evaluating 15 full cross-attention forward passes on CPU takes 13.8ms total (~15ms per candidate passage),
     which is normal, expected CPU forward-pass compute for 15 transformer cross-encoder inferences.
